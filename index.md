## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in **Biomedical Engineering at Yale University**, advised by Professor [Evelyn Lake](https://medicine.yale.edu/profile/evelyn-lake/) in Radiology & Biomedical Imaging. I develop multimodal imaging approaches that connect cellular physiology with whole-brain functional dynamics.

My current work combines wide-field optical microscopy with functional MRI to study brain function across scales. Before Yale, I investigated cardiac electrophysiology, cross-organ metabolism, and intestinal biology at Stanford and UC Irvine. Across these systems, I am interested in engineering rigorous measurements that translate between molecular mechanisms and organ-level function.

## Research Interests

- **Multimodal imaging:** Simultaneous optical and MR measurements across spatial and temporal scales.
- **Systems neuroscience:** Brain-wide signatures of physiology, behavior, and disease in translational models.
- **Quantitative biology:** Integrated tools for reproducible, mechanistic biological measurement.
